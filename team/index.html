The people behind the roast

Meet the team

Small team, long days, very good coffee.

Team members

  • Mara Ellison

    Founder and head roaster

    Mara started Tidewell in a rented garage with a four-pound roaster and an opinion about freshness. She still roasts every Thursday batch herself and cups every lot before it ships.

  • Theo Park

    Green coffee buyer

    Theo spends about ten weeks a year at origin, walking farms and tasting lots. He builds the long relationships that let us name the farm behind every bag.

  • Iris Okafor

    Head of wholesale

    Iris helps cafes, restaurants and offices build coffee programs that fit their volume, equipment and customers, and trains their teams to serve it well.

  • Jonah Reyes

    Lead barista and trainer

    Jonah runs our Saturday classes and writes most of our brewing guides. If a recipe is in the journal, he has brewed it a few hundred times.
