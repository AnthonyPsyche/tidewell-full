A good pour-over is mostly repetition. Once you fix the variables, you can taste the effect of every change. Here is the recipe we teach in our Saturday class.

## What you need

- A pour-over brewer and paper filters
- A scale and a timer
- Fresh whole beans and a burr grinder
- A kettle

## The recipe

1. **Weigh 15 grams of coffee** and grind it to the texture of coarse sand.
2. **Rinse the filter** with hot water, then discard the water. This removes paper taste and warms the brewer.
3. **Bloom for 30 seconds.** Pour 45 grams of water over the grounds and wait. Fresh coffee will bubble as it releases gas.
4. **Pour in slow circles** until you reach 250 grams total, finishing by about 2 minutes 30.
5. **Let it drain** fully. The whole brew should take about 3 minutes.

## Troubleshooting

If the cup tastes sour or thin, grind finer or pour slower. If it tastes bitter or harsh, grind coarser or lower the water temperature a little. Change one thing at a time.
