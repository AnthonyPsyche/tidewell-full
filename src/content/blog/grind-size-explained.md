If you can only change one thing about your brewing, change the grind. It controls how fast water pulls flavor out of the coffee.

## The rule of thumb

Finer grinds extract faster and make stronger, sometimes bitter coffee. Coarser grinds extract slower and can taste sour or thin.

## Settings by brewer

- **Espresso:** fine, like table salt
- **Pour-over and drip:** medium, like coarse sand
- **French press:** coarse, like breadcrumbs
- **Cold brew:** extra coarse

## Dialing it in

Taste, adjust one step, and brew again. Write down the setting that works so you can return to it.
