Northwind's break room ran on pods that nobody liked and everybody used.

## What we installed

A commercial bean-to-cup machine, filtered water, and a standing delivery of fresh beans every two weeks.

## What they said

The office manager spends about an hour a month on it, and the team now stops for coffee instead of walking to a cafe.
