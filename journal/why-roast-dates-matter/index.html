Why freshness matters: the case for roast dates

Why a roast date beats a best-before date, how long coffee stays at its best, and how to store beans so you get the most from every bag.

By Mara Ellison · September 30, 2026 · 1 min read

Look at the back of most coffee bags and you will find a best-before date a year away. That tells you almost nothing about how the coffee will taste.

What roast dates tell you

Coffee is at its best within a few weeks of roasting. A roast date lets you buy what is fresh and brew it at its peak.

Resting is real

Right after roasting, beans release a lot of carbon dioxide. Giving them a few days to settle makes for a calmer, more even extraction.

How to store it

Keep beans in an airtight container away from light, heat and moisture. Buy what you will use in a few weeks and grind just before brewing.

Frequently asked questions

How long after roasting is coffee best?

Most coffees taste their best between 4 and 21 days after roasting, once the initial burst of gas has settled and before the aromas fade.

Should I keep coffee in the freezer?

For long storage, freeze in small airtight portions and thaw sealed. For everyday use, an airtight container in a cool, dark cupboard is better.
