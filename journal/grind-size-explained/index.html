Grind size, explained

Grind size is the fastest way to fix a cup that tastes sour or bitter. Learn what each grind does and the right setting for every brewer.

By Jonah Reyes · September 18, 2026 · 1 min read

If you can only change one thing about your brewing, change the grind. It controls how fast water pulls flavor out of the coffee.

The rule of thumb

Finer grinds extract faster and make stronger, sometimes bitter coffee. Coarser grinds extract slower and can taste sour or thin.

Settings by brewer

  • Espresso: fine, like table salt
  • Pour-over and drip: medium, like coarse sand
  • French press: coarse, like breadcrumbs
  • Cold brew: extra coarse

Dialing it in

Taste, adjust one step, and brew again. Write down the setting that works so you can return to it.

Frequently asked questions

Is a burr grinder really necessary?

It makes the biggest difference of any upgrade, because burrs produce a consistent particle size and blades do not.

What grind for a French press?

Coarse, like breadcrumbs. A finer grind will over-extract and clog the filter.
