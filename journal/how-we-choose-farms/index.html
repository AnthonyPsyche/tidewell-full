How we choose the farms we buy from

How our buyer picks farms and lots: the cupping scores, relationships and traceability that decide which coffees make it into a Tidewell bag.

By Theo Park · October 8, 2026 · 1 min read

Every Tidewell coffee starts as a conversation, not a catalog. Here is how a lot earns a place in our lineup.

We taste first

Samples arrive green and we roast and cup them blind. Only lots that score well and taste distinct move to the next step.

We meet the people

A great cup can come from one good harvest. We want farms that can deliver it again, so we visit, walk the plots and learn how the coffee is grown and processed.

We keep it traceable

Each bag names its origin. We publish the farm, the region and the harvest year, so you know exactly what you are drinking.

We pay for quality

Direct relationships let us pay farmers more for better coffee, and that keeps good producers growing it.

Frequently asked questions

What does traceable coffee mean?

It means we can tell you the farm or cooperative, the region and the harvest behind each bag, and what we paid for it.

Do you pay above fair trade prices?

We negotiate directly and pay a premium over commodity prices for quality. The exact premium varies by lot and is shared with partners on request.
