Pier Nine Roast Bar · Cedar Point, OR

Pier Nine Roast Bar launched a house blend in six weeks

A new restaurant wanted its own house coffee. We built a custom blend, a menu, and a training plan before opening night.

Results

From brief to first pour
6 wks
Blends tested
3
Guests who ordered coffee
92%

Pier Nine opened with a simple brief: a house coffee that tastes like the room feels.

The process

We cupped three blends with the owners, tweaked the winning one for their espresso machine, and wrote a one-page recipe card for every shift.

The result

Guests ask about the coffee by name, and the restaurant now sells bags at the counter.
