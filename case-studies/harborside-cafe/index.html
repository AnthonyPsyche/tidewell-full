Harborside Cafe · Port Alder, WA

Harborside Cafe cut waste by a third with right-sized orders

How a busy waterfront cafe moved to twice-weekly small deliveries, trained its team on pour-over, and cut stale coffee waste.

Results

Less coffee waste
31%
Weekly deliveries
2x
Average customer rating
4.8

Harborside Cafe was ordering large bags once a month and throwing out the last third. Their baristas liked the coffee but didn’t know how to dial in a new roast.

What we did

We moved them to twice-weekly small deliveries sized to their real volume, and Jonah spent a morning training the team on grind, ratio and timing.

What changed

The cafe now serves coffee that is always days old, and the menu gets a rotating single-origin pour-over that regulars order by name.
